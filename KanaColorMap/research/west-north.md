# West and north ESO zone colour evidence

> Research scope: 15 actual ESO surface zones, including Grayhome (map 1864). Native extracted maps/contact sheets and the listed screenshot pixels were viewed. Search-result image descriptions were not treated as visual evidence. Named sources below distinguish directly identified locations from uncaptioned viewpoints.

These hex values are proposed muted artist swatches inferred from the photographs, not sampled texture values. Weather, sunset, night, colour grading and parchment affect appearance; the goal is daylight terrain identity. Use the live native blob masks and coordinates in `native-blobs.json` for world placement. Zone-map coordinates describe internal orientation and must be fitted to each native world footprint; do not relocate land or redraw coastlines from lore maps.

Map sheets inspected: `/private/tmp/kana-color-map-source/zones-reference-1.png`, `zones-reference-2.png`, `zones-reference-3.png`. Individual extracted-map links below identify their originals. No claim is made that every small biome pocket was photographed; limitations are explicit for each zone.

## Glenumbra — map 1

**Palette:** #617044 · #87925A · #A19563 · #777D73 · #454F43.

**Geography:** Long southwest–northeast peninsula; Daggerfall southwest, Beldama Wyrd southern interior, Camlorn central, craggy northern spine. Ocean remains outside the native land mask.

- **Daenia / Beldama Wyrd / Daggerfall (south and southwest):** Temperate green and yellow-green broadleaf woodland with grassy openings; Daggerfall coastal stone city. Colours: #617044, #87925A, #777D73.
- **Cambray Hills / Westtry (central):** Rolling mixed woodland; Westtry is locally grey, sparse and dead-wooded. Do not extend its blight across all Glenumbra. Colours: #87925A, #A19563, #777D73.
- **Hag Fen and northern uplands (east-central wet lowland; rocky northern/northeastern spine):** Dark wet lowland inferred from native map; upland crags and forest farther north. Preserve the native peninsula and bays. Colours: #454F43, #617044, #777D73.
