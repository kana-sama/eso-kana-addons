# South and western islands: terrain color evidence

Research date: 2026-09-27. Scope: general Tamriel map painting only. All 14 supplied native zone maps were visually inspected in the extracted contact sheets; Norg-Tzel exterior map was separately viewed online. Every zone below also has at least two actual in-game screenshots visually inspected through the browser (Summerset uses a multi-location gallery). Screenshots are observation evidence; UESP prose supplies names and cardinal placement. Concept/loading art, image-search generated descriptions, and blocked images are not counted as viewed screenshot evidence.

Hex values are hand-chosen rendering palettes grounded in observed landcover, not measured physical RGB or pixel samples. Raise daytime greens/sands moderately for legibility; do not copy night/rain/haze tint as terrain. Preserve original coastline, watercourses and shaded relief. Do not add giant decorative mountains. When a subregion was not separately photographed, the gap is explicit. Source links include the exact screenshot rather than only a general website.
