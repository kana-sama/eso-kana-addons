# Central and eastern Tamriel: visual color evidence

Research date: 2026-09-27. All hex palettes below are deliberately brighter cartographic interpretations of observed terrain, not sampled pixel measurements. Native parchment yellow is not itself a biome color. Terrain shapes must come from the native zone/world maps, and existing native playable-zone blobs define the only permitted painted footprint.

## Method and limits

Viewed native contact sheets 1–4 (all listed zones); also inspected the full native Vvardenfell and West Weald textures. Viewed 24 region-specific screenshots in the browser. `viewed: true` in JSON means the image itself was rendered and inspected, not merely its search description. Sources without exact geographic captions are explicitly named as such: their colors support a zone-wide palette but cannot establish a precise subzone boundary. UESP article pages returned 403, while many discovered UESP image URLs were viewable. No downloaded art is a game texture deliverable.

Imperial City has no separate native world-map blob in the parent’s live export; its city palette is only a tiny detail within Cyrodiil. No inaccessible land should be inferred or recolored.
